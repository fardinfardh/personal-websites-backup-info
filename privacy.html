Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Purpose

Personal Websites Backup is a private-use system operated by the account owner to back up websites and their associated data.

Google Drive Access

The system uses Google Drive authorization to create, read, update, and manage website backup files in the owner's authorized Google Drive account.

Data Handling

Backup files may contain website source code, configuration information, and application records. These files are stored in the owner's Google Drive account and are not intentionally made public.

Google user data is used only to perform and maintain authorized backup operations. It is not sold to third parties or used for advertising.

Storage and Retention

Backups are maintained until replaced or removed by the account owner or the authorized backup system. Some backup operations may replace earlier copies.

Security

Access is managed through Google OAuth authorization and credentials stored in authorized environments.

Revoking Access

The account owner may revoke Google Drive access through their Google Account security settings.
